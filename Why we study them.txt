Why we study them?

1. Explains Natural Phenomena:

 - Weather patterns, such as: water cycle.

 - plasma physics, such as: Formation of stars and the behavior of the Sun.

 - Earth's structure, for example: solid crust and liquid mantle.

2. Important for Engineering and Technology:

 - Designing materials, such as: metals, plastics and gels.

 - Understanding fluid flow in pipelines, engines, and aircraft.

 - Plasma is used in electronics manufacturing.

3. Essential in Chemistry and Physics

 - Studying phase changes, like: melting, boiling and sublimation.

 - Understanding how energy affects matter.

 - Helping predict reactions and material behavior.

4. Vital in Medicine and Industry:

 - Medical imaging uses plasma technology.

 - Gases are used in anesthesia, which is Loss of sensation or awareness, often induced for medical procedures.

 - Liquids are used for chemical solutions.

5. Energy and Environmental Applications

 - Plasma used in clean energy, for example: fusion research.

 - Gases play a major role in climate science.

 - Liquid behavior important in water treatment systems.
