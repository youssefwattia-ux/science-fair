Liquids have particles that stay close together but can slide past each other, giving them a fixed volume but no fixed shape. They flow, adapt, and take the shape of their container.

Key Properties:

 - No fixed shape

 - Fixed volume

 - High flowability

 - Almost incompressible compared to solids

Moral Lesson:

 - Be flexible and flow around obstacles like a liquid!

 - Adapt, but stay true to your volume!
