Plasma is an energetic state where atoms are ionized, meaning particles carry electric charges. It conducts electricity, glows, and is found in stars, lightning, and neon signs.

Key Properties:

 - Ionized particles

 - Extremely high energy

 - Conducts electricity

 - Emits light
