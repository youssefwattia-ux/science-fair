The Four States of Matter:

 - Solid has a fixed shape and volume; it is strong and stable

 - Liquid takes the shape of its container; it is flexible and flowing

 - Gas spreads freely; it is free and expansive

 - Plasma is highly energetic and glowing; it is bright and powerful

Why Study Them?
 - Understanding matter helps explain nature, design materials, and improve technology

Life Advice:
 - Be steady like a solid, adapt like a liquid, expand like a gas, and shine like plasma!
